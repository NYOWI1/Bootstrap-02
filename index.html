Karina (aespa)

About Me

Karina is a South Korean singer, rapper, and dancer under SM Entertainment. She debuted as the leader of the girl group aespa in 2020. She is known for her powerful stage presence, elegant visuals, and excellent dance performances. Karina has gained worldwide popularity through aespa's successful songs and international concerts.

Achievements

  • Leader of the K-pop girl group aespa.
  • Debuted with the hit single "Black Mamba" in 2020.
  • Won multiple music awards with aespa, including Song of the Year and Best Female Group.
  • Performed at major international music festivals and world tours.
  • Recognized as one of the most influential fourth-generation K-pop idols.
